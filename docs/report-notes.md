# Report notes

## Overview

Restaurant listings from the supplied Zomato dataset. Values reflect the current filters.

## Markets

Index: 1.00 = selected market; above 1.00 = above its average. Cuisine counts overlap.

## Services & Pricing

India costs are shown in INR; zero costs are excluded. Ratings are kept as supplied.

## Delivery Gaps

With / no delivery columns refer to restaurants with at least 100 votes. Hover over a bar for more detail.

## Restaurant List

Restaurant ID identifies each record. Select a country or city to narrow the list.
